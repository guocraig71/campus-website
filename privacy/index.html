关于本政策About this policy

本政策说明 Campus 如何处理你使用 App 时涉及的数据。Campus 由独立开发者维护。隐私问题、访问、更正或删除请求，请联系 guocraig71@gmail.com。

Campus 不使用广告、跨 App 跟踪或第三方分析 SDK，也不出售个人数据。官网不设置分析 Cookie 或跟踪脚本；托管服务仍会处理提供网页所需的网络信息。

This policy explains how Campus handles data involved in using the app. Campus is maintained by an independent developer. For privacy questions or requests for access, correction or deletion, email guocraig71@gmail.com.

Campus does not use advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not sell personal data. This website sets no analytics cookies or tracking scripts. Its hosting provider still processes network information needed to deliver pages.

本机记录与存储On-device records and storage

课程、个人日程、待办标题和备注、截止时间、优先级、完成历史、消费记录、生活费额度、航班与高铁行程、附件、背景图片和偏好保存在设备中。记录使用本地数据库，附件存放在本地文件中；偏好使用系统偏好存储，订阅链接与服务凭据使用本设备 Keychain。

普通截图和 PDF 文字识别使用 Apple Vision 和 PDFKit 在本机完成。删除或替换附件会移除不再被使用的本机文件；已导出的副本需要单独删除。正常切后台返回时保留编辑状态，仍需点保存才写入行程；不因后台切换而自动保存或新增上传。

当前版本不提供 Campus 跨设备云同步。 Mac 版与 iPhone 使用独立的本机存储，转移数据需自行导出并恢复备份。Mac 文件选择与拖放仅访问你明确选择的文件；小组件通过本机共享容器读取必要摘要，不共享原始票据或完整数据库。Mac 的进行中状态显示在 App 内，退出 App 后不继续刷新；通知由系统权限控制，不提供灵动岛。系统备份可能包含本地数据，由 iOS 设置管理；App 的备份导出和恢复由你主动操作，导出文件可能包含个人资料，请自行保管。

Classes, personal events, task titles and notes, due times, priorities, completion history, expenses, allowances, flights, train journeys, attachments, background images and preferences are stored on your device. Records use a local database and attachments use local files. Preferences use system preference storage; subscription links and service credentials use this device’s Keychain.

Standard screenshot and PDF text recognition uses Apple Vision and PDFKit on device. Deleting or replacing an attachment removes locally unused files. Delete separately exported copies yourself. Editing state is retained during a normal background switch, but journeys are written only when you tap Save. Switching apps does not automatically save a journey or start an upload.

The current version does not provide Campus cross-device cloud sync. Mac and iPhone use separate local storage; transfer data explicitly by exporting and restoring a backup. Mac file selection and drag and drop access only files you choose. Widgets read necessary summaries through a local shared container, without original tickets or the full database. On Mac, ongoing status appears inside the app and stops refreshing after Quit; notifications depend on system permission and there is no Dynamic Island. iOS backups may include local data and are controlled in system settings. App backup export and restore are initiated by you. Exported files may contain personal information; keep them safe.

权限与系统集成Permissions and system integrations

读取 Apple 日历需系统的完整日历访问权限。Campus 仅读取你选中的日历，不修改系统日历事件。Campus 的 Reminders 是本机待办，不读取或同步 Apple 提醒事项。

照片导入与自定义背景使用系统照片选择器，仅接收选中的图片。点按麦克风时才申请并使用麦克风和语音识别权限；App 使用设备端 Apple Speech 识别，不保存录音，不支持时可手动输入。iOS 键盘听写由系统提供，其联网行为由系统设置决定。App 没有直接拍照、定位、通讯录或广告跟踪功能。

通知需要单独授权，可按类别关闭。通知、实时活动和本机 App Group 小组件摘要可能显示任务标题、时间、地点和生活费摘要；请通过通知预览和相关设置控制锁屏可见性。小组件不共享完整账本、备注或附件。拒绝可选权限仍可手动管理记录。

具备签名能力的版本可提供 Apple 登录，仅使用授权的标识、邮箱和名字作为本机资料；不读取设备当前 Apple ID，也不提供跨设备资料同步。退出可能保留同一账户的姓名缓存，删除全部个人数据会清除。

Reading Apple Calendar requires full calendar-access permission. Campus reads only calendars you select and does not modify their events. Campus Reminders are local tasks; the app does not read or sync Apple Reminders.

Photo import and custom backgrounds use the system photo picker and receive only selected images. Microphone and speech recognition access are requested and used after you tap the microphone. Campus uses Apple Speech on-device recognition and does not save recordings; manual entry is available when unsupported. iOS keyboard dictation is provided by the system, whose settings govern its network behavior. Campus has no direct camera capture, location, contacts or advertising-tracking feature.

Notifications require separate permission and can be disabled by type. Notifications, Live Activities and local App Group widget summaries may display task titles, times, locations and allowance summaries. Control Lock Screen visibility through notification previews and related settings. Widgets do not share the full ledger, notes or attachments. Manual record management remains available without optional permissions.

Capable signed builds may offer Sign in with Apple, using the authorized identifier, email and given name for a local profile. Campus does not read the device’s current Apple ID or provide cross-device profile sync. Sign-out may retain the same account’s cached name; deleting all personal data clears it.

可选智能辅助Optional AI assistance

自然语言输入默认使用本机规则解析。支持时，Apple 设备端系统语言模型可辅助填写名称、日期和时间，此路径不向 Campus 云端发送文字或音频。结果只是可编辑草稿，需你确认后保存；模型不可用时仍可使用规则或手动输入。

云端日期辅助需要你启用并预览、确认发送。设备只发送经过筛选的日期词语、当前日期和时区，Cloudflare Workers AI 托管的 GLM 模型用于推断日期和时间。原始标题、姓名、地点、历史日程和附件不随此日期请求发送。请求同时使用随机服务会话进行鉴权，并受限流和配额约束。

机票与高铁票云端识别是另一项可选功能，需要单独同意。设备将选中的截图或 PDF 最多四页转换为压缩 JPEG 票图，通过 HTTPS 发送至 Campus 后端与 Cloudflare Workers AI 的 Qwen 视觉模型；不上传原始 PDF 或文件元数据。票面仍可能含姓名、证件信息、订单号或二维码，请先遮挡不需要的信息。模型提取行程字段，直接填入可编辑表单；缺失或无效字段留空，不因识别失败而自动使用本机票图结果。请手动核对后保存到本机。

Campus 的识别实现不把 AI 输入、票图或输出正文写入应用数据库、缓存或应用日志，并在模型请求中设置 store=false。服务仍需临时处理输入输出；Cloudflare 的网络、安全、基础设施处理及适用保留规则由其政策约束，不能将此描述为第三方绝不保留任何信息。关闭辅助或票图识别会停止后续请求，不自动删除已有服务会话。

Natural-language input uses local rules by default. When available, Apple’s on-device system language model can help fill a title, date and time without sending text or audio to Campus cloud services. Results are editable drafts requiring your confirmation before saving. Local rules and manual entry remain available when the model is unavailable.

Cloud date assistance requires enabling it, previewing and confirming the request. Your device sends only filtered date words, the current date and time zone to a GLM model hosted by Cloudflare Workers AI to infer date and time. Original titles, names, places, schedule history and attachments are not sent with this date request. A random service session authenticates requests, which are subject to rate limits and quotas.

Cloud flight and train-ticket recognition is a separate optional feature requiring separate consent. Your device converts selected screenshots or up to four PDF pages into resized JPEG ticket images and sends them over HTTPS to the Campus backend and a Qwen vision model on Cloudflare Workers AI. Original PDFs and file metadata are not uploaded. Images may still contain names, identity details, booking numbers or QR codes; cover unnecessary details first. The model extracts journey fields into an editable form. Missing or invalid fields remain empty, and failed recognition does not automatically apply local ticket results. Review and complete the fields before saving locally.

The Campus recognition implementation does not write AI inputs, ticket images or output bodies to application databases, caches or application logs, and sets store=false in model requests. Inputs and outputs must still be processed temporarily. Cloudflare’s network, security and infrastructure processing and applicable retention are governed by its policies; this is not a promise that third parties retain no information. Disabling assistance or ticket recognition stops new requests but does not automatically delete an existing service session.

联网与第三方服务Network requests and providers

参考汇率请求直接发送至 Frankfurter，只包含币种和参考日期，不包含消费金额、商家或个人账户。新获取的参考汇率使用欧洲中央银行来源，交叉汇率和消费金额在本机计算，不代表银行实际扣款；历史记录可能保留旧版来源的换算。

连接学校课表时，设备访问你提供的 Canvas、Moodle 或 ICS 地址，对方会收到链接和必要网络信息。请仅使用你有权访问的订阅。服务商和网页托管方会处理请求所需的 IP 等网络信息，处理地区可能不同于你所在地；联网传输使用 HTTPS。

本次发行范围不包括 Google OAuth、行程邮箱接收、远程 APNs 和在线航空状态补全。源码中的开发测试路径不代表发行功能。当前日期与票图辅助使用 Cloudflare Workers AI；本次发行不使用 Google Gemini 处理智能添加。

Reference-rate requests go directly to Frankfurter and contain only currency codes and reference dates, not expense amounts, merchants or personal accounts. Newly fetched rates use European Central Bank data. Cross-rates and expense conversions are calculated locally and are not actual bank settlement amounts. Historical records may retain conversions from an earlier source.

For school feeds, your device requests the Canvas, Moodle or ICS address you provide. That provider receives the URL and necessary network information. Use only subscriptions you are authorized to access. Service and website hosting providers process network information such as IP addresses needed for requests. Processing may occur outside your region; network transmission uses HTTPS.

This release scope does not include Google OAuth, trip-email reception, remote APNs or online flight-status completion. Development paths in source code are not release features. Date and ticket assistance use Cloudflare Workers AI; this release does not use Google Gemini for smart add.

服务端记录与保留Server records and retention

你确认发送云端辅助请求后，Campus 创建随机服务会话。随机用户标识、会话散列及既有旧版本连接元数据保留至删除。会话授权最长一年有效，这不等于一年后自动删除资料。当前发行不上传系统日历或学校课表连接元数据。

后端对 API 请求 IP 作 SHA-256 散列用于限流，计数窗口为一分钟或一小时，AI 用户计数窗口最长一天。散列不等于完全匿名。过期计数由每小时维护任务清理，服务停机可能延迟清理。成功删除后的会话散列回执保留七天供重试，再由维护任务移除。

旧版共享航班查询缓存不含乘机人或订单字段，最后更新七天后由每小时维护清理；当前发行不新增在线航班查询。服务端资料存放于 Cloudflare D1。删除实时记录后，平台灾备历史可能暂存副本：免费计划回溯七天，付费计划最长三十天。灾备副本不能作为重新启用已删除账户的理由。

基础设施、安全与网络信息的处理仍受 Cloudflare 政策约束。服务会话与限流记录是云端资料，因此 Campus 不声称所有数据完全本地处理。

After you confirm a cloud-assistance request, Campus creates a random service session. Random user identifiers, session hashes and any historical connection metadata remain until deletion. Session authorization lasts up to one year; this is not automatic record deletion after a year. This release does not upload native-calendar or school-feed connection metadata.

The backend SHA-256 hashes API request IP addresses for rate limiting, using one-minute or one-hour windows; AI user counters use windows up to one day. Hashing does not guarantee anonymity. Hourly maintenance removes expired counters; outages may delay cleanup. A session-hash receipt remains for seven days after successful deletion to support retries, then maintenance removes it.

Historical shared flight-query caches contain no passenger or booking fields and are removed by hourly maintenance seven days after their last update. This release creates no new online flight queries. Server records use Cloudflare D1. After live records are deleted, disaster-recovery history may retain copies: seven days on the free plan or up to thirty days on a paid plan. Recovery copies must not be used to reactivate deleted accounts.

Infrastructure, security and network processing remains subject to Cloudflare policies. Service sessions and rate-limit records are cloud data; Campus does not claim all processing takes place locally.

删除、撤回与个人权利Deletion, withdrawal and your rights

设置中的“删除账户与全部个人数据”会删除本设备记录、附件、恢复副本、订阅凭据、Apple 身份与姓名缓存、已排定通知和实时活动，并请求删除既有服务会话关联的服务端记录。没有服务会话时仅作本机删除，不为删除而创建账户。联网删除失败会保留重试所需凭据，不视为服务端删除已完成。

单条记录删除不会自动删除旧恢复副本。你导出的备份、iOS 备份和其他设备副本需要分别处理。卸载 App 不等于删除旧服务端账户；退出也不等于删除全部本地资料。

你可以断开订阅、关闭通知与实时活动，或在 iOS 设置撤回日历、麦克风、语音识别和通知权限。关闭智能辅助只停止新请求；清除已有服务会话需执行删除操作。适用的访问、更正、删除、限制处理及撤回请求可通过支持邮箱提出。

“Delete account and all personal data” in Settings removes device records, attachments, recovery copies, subscription credentials, Apple identity and name caches, scheduled notifications and Live Activities. It also requests deletion of server records linked to an existing service session. With no session, deletion is local only and does not create an account. Failed online deletion preserves credentials needed to retry and is not treated as completed server deletion.

Deleting one record does not automatically remove older recovery copies. Manage exported backups, iOS backups and other-device copies separately. Uninstalling the app does not delete an older server account; signing out does not delete all local records.

Disconnect subscriptions, disable notifications and Live Activities, or revoke calendar, microphone, speech recognition and notification access in iOS Settings. Disabling AI assistance only stops new requests; use deletion to clear an existing service session. Email support for applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal requests.

支持邮件与网站访问Support emails and website visits

主动发送支持邮件时，邮件地址、正文和附件会通过你的邮件服务与开发者使用的 Gmail 处理，用于回复、排查问题和处理请求。请只提供必要信息。邮件会保留至处理问题所需的期限；你可联系请求删除，但适用法律或安全需要可能要求保留部分记录。

官网静态页面由所选托管服务提供。使用 GitHub Pages 时，GitHub 会处理访问所需的网络信息，其保留与处理适用 GitHub 隐私声明。官网不设账户、客服后台或在线提交表单。

If you send a support email, your address, message and attachments are processed by your email provider and the developer’s Gmail service to reply, investigate and handle requests. Share only what is necessary. Emails are kept as needed to resolve the matter. You may request deletion, though applicable legal or security requirements may require some records to be retained.

Static website pages are delivered by the chosen hosting provider. When GitHub Pages is used, GitHub processes network information needed for visits under its privacy statement. This website has no accounts, support backend or online submission form.

可选 Gmail 机票邮件导入Optional Gmail flight-mail import

Gmail 连接是独立可选功能，使用 Google 官方 OAuth 和 Gmail API 的只读权限；该权限允许读取邮箱，Campus 只查询最近90天可能涉及航班预订的邮件，每次最多检查50封。相关邮件正文（纯文本或HTML）及必要邮件头由 Google 传给 Campus 的 Cloudflare 服务端临时解析；不读取附件内容，不保存或记录原文，不发送给 AI 服务或用于广告、模型训练。解析只产生可编辑候选，确认后才写入本机行程。

Gmail 授权凭据以AES-GCM加密保存在服务端，用于在你发起同步时读取相关邮件。服务端保存随机连接标识、邮件标识及提取的航班号、机场、日期时间和时区，不保存邮件原文；候选缓存下次同步时替换，断开／删除连接或删除账户时清理。OAuth临时状态最长10分钟，完成后一次性消费；过期状态在后续授权维护时清理。

Gmail 可在连接页断开或删除；Campus 清除服务端授权凭据和候选，并尝试向 Google 撤销授权。你也可在 Google 账户的第三方连接中撤销 Campus 访问。断开时可选择保留或删除本机已导入行程；不会删除或修改 Gmail 原邮件。

Gmail 当前采用 Google 外部测试模式，只有已配置的测试用户可以连接，尚未完成 Google 公开发行验证。Campus 对从 Google API 接收的信息的使用和转移遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括 Limited Use 要求。

Gmail is separately optional and uses Google OAuth and the Gmail API readonly scope. This scope permits mailbox reading; Campus queries recent 90-day messages likely related to flight bookings and checks at most 50 per sync. Google sends matching plain-text/HTML bodies and necessary headers to the Campus Cloudflare service for transient parsing. Attachments are not read, raw mail is not stored or logged, and mail is not sent to AI or used for advertising or model training. Editable candidates require confirmation before local import.

Gmail authorization tokens are encrypted with AES-GCM on the server for sync requests you initiate. Server records include random connection identifiers, message identifiers and extracted flight numbers, airports, dates/times and time zones, without raw email. Candidate caches are replaced on sync and cleared on disconnect, connection deletion or account deletion. Temporary OAuth state is valid up to ten minutes, consumed once and expired entries are cleaned during subsequent authorization maintenance.

Disconnect or delete Gmail in Connections to clear server tokens/candidates and attempt Google token revocation. You may also revoke Campus in your Google Account third-party connections. Choose whether to keep or remove imported local journeys when disconnecting. Campus never deletes or modifies original Gmail messages.

Gmail currently uses Google external testing and is available only to configured test users; Google public-release verification is not complete. Campus use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

儿童、同意与政策更新Children, consent and policy changes

Campus 面向管理个人学习与生活的用户，不主动索取儿童资料。监护人可联系请求处理或删除相关资料。同意隐私政策不等于授予系统权限，也不自动开启云端功能；权限和云端发送分别确认。

实质性数据处理变化时会更新说明并在需要时重新取得同意。最新说明也可在 App 设置中查看。第三方名称仅用于识别服务，没有官方合作或背书。行程卡不能代替登机或乘车凭证。

Campus is intended for managing personal study and everyday life and does not intentionally solicit children’s data. Guardians may contact us about handling or deleting relevant data. Agreement to a privacy policy does not grant system permissions or enable cloud features; permissions and cloud requests are confirmed separately.

Material data-practice changes will be reflected in updated notices and renewed consent when needed. The current notice is also available in app Settings. Third-party names identify services and do not imply partnership or endorsement. Journey cards do not replace boarding or travel credentials.
