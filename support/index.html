常见问题Common questions

Campus 可以做什么？What can I do with Campus?
Campus 帮你集中查看当天安排，管理日程与本地待办，记录开支和月生活费。当前版本适用于 iOS 26 或更新版本。App Store 下载尚未开放。Campus brings together your daily overview, schedules, local tasks, expenses and monthly allowance. The current build requires iOS 26 or later. App Store downloads are not yet available.
为什么 Apple 日历没有显示？Why are my Apple calendars missing?
在 Calendar 的“连接日历”中连接 Apple 日历并选择需要显示的日历。Campus 需要完整日历访问权限，仅写入权限不足以读取。若曾拒绝授权，请在 iOS 设置中找到 Campus，检查日历权限。确认对应日历在系统日历 App 中仍然存在；已删除或失效的日历需要重新选择。临时读取错误可点击“重试”，内容过多时可减少选中的日历。系统日历是只读连接，手动创建的 Campus 日程不会写回 Apple 日历。In Calendar, open “Connect calendars”, connect Apple Calendar and select the calendars you want to display. Full calendar access is needed; write-only access cannot read events. If you previously declined permission, find Campus in iOS Settings and check calendar access. Make sure the calendar still exists in Apple Calendar and select it again if it was removed or became unavailable. Use “Retry” for a temporary read error, or select fewer calendars if the content is too large. This connection is read-only; events created in Campus are not written back to Apple Calendar.
为什么待办没有显示？Why can’t I see a task?
先检查 Reminders 的“未完成”“今天”“已完成”筛选，以及任务的截止日期。已完成的任务在“已完成”中；没有截止日期的任务不一定出现在“今天”。Campus 待办与 Apple 提醒事项是不同的数据，不读取或同步 Apple 提醒事项，因此不需要开启该系统权限。Check the Open, Today and Done filters in Reminders, along with the task’s due date. Completed tasks appear under Done; tasks without a due date may not appear under Today. Campus tasks are separate from Apple Reminders. Campus does not read or sync that app’s tasks, so no Apple Reminders permission is required.
为什么没有收到通知？Why didn’t I receive a notification?
检查任务或日程是否设置了提醒，以及 Campus 中相关通知是否开启。再检查 iOS 设置中的通知授权、专注模式和通知预览。通知由设备本地调度；系统可能影响展示时间与可见性。拒绝通知权限不会删除已保存的记录。Check that the event or task has a reminder and that the relevant notifications are enabled in Campus. Then check notification permission, Focus and notification previews in iOS Settings. Notifications are scheduled locally; iOS can affect when and how they are displayed. Declining notification permission does not delete saved records.
智能添加失败怎么办？What if smart add doesn’t work?
你可以继续使用本机识别或手动填写。Apple 设备端模型取决于设备、语言和系统可用性。云端日期辅助需启用、预览并确认发送，且需要网络和可用额度；服务繁忙、限流或超时可能导致失败，请稍后再试。识别结果不会自动保存，请核对标题、日期和时间后再保存。Continue with local parsing or manual entry. Apple’s on-device model depends on device, language and system availability. Cloud date assistance requires enabling it, previewing and confirming the request, a network connection and available quota. Busy services, rate limits or timeouts can cause a failure; try again later. Results are never saved automatically. Review the title, date and time before saving.
票图识别与乘车码有什么区别？How does ticket recognition differ from a boarding code?
机票或高铁票的云端识别需单独同意上传选中的压缩图片，结果仍需核对。不要上传与识别无关的证件信息，尽量先裁切或遮挡。独立的乘车码入口仅在本机保存和预览选中的图片或 PDF，不进行 AI 识别、验票或二维码解码；乘车码可能是出行凭证，请妥善保管。Cloud flight or train-ticket recognition requires separate consent to upload the selected resized images. Review the results. Crop or cover unnecessary identity details before uploading. The separate boarding-code entry saves and previews selected images or PDFs locally; it does not use AI, validate tickets or decode QR codes. A boarding code may be a travel credential, so keep it safe.
消费换算一直显示待处理？Why is an expense conversion pending?
参考汇率需要网络连接和数据源提供对应日期、币种的汇率。换算失败不会删除原始消费记录；网络恢复后 App 会尝试补齐，也可使用界面中的“重试待处理换算”。参考金额可能与银行实际扣款不同。Reference conversions need an internet connection and a rate for the requested date and currency. A failed conversion does not delete the original expense. Campus attempts to fill missing conversions when the connection returns; you can also use “Retry pending conversions”. Reference amounts may differ from actual bank charges.
如何添加机票或高铁行程？How do I add a flight or train journey?
在 Calendar 右上角加号选择“旅行行程”，再用右上角加号或“添加行程”选择机票／高铁。可直接填写，或在单独同意后扫描截图／PDF。识别结果填入同一页可编辑表单，手动补充缺失信息后点保存。识别失败不会自动改用本机票图识别结果，可重试或手动填写。In Calendar, use the top-right plus and choose Travel itineraries. Use the Travel plus or Add a trip to choose a flight or train. Enter fields manually or scan a screenshot/PDF after separate consent. Results fill the same editable form; complete missing fields and tap Save. A failed scan does not automatically apply local ticket-recognition results; retry or enter fields manually.
为什么行程在历史记录里，或日期没有填入？Why is a journey in History, or its date missing?
请核对票面完整年份、月份、日期与出发／到达时间。云端识别不接受 2026 年以前的票据日期，也不会把旧年份直接改成 2026；未能确认的信息留空。高铁票使用北京时间（UTC+8）。未来出发的行程应在 Upcoming；已结束的行程在 History。没有到达时间的高铁行程在出发当天保留。历史行程可打开查看、编辑或删除。Check the full year, month, day and departure/arrival times against the ticket. Cloud recognition rejects ticket dates before 2026 and does not rewrite an old year as 2026; uncertain fields remain empty. Train tickets use Beijing time (UTC+8). Future departures belong in Upcoming and finished journeys in History. Trains without an arrival time remain current through their departure day. Open a history item to view, edit or delete it.
识别一直等待，或提示额度用尽怎么办？What if recognition waits or reaches its quota?
进度条表示准备图片、等待 AI 和完成阶段，不是模型计算的精确百分比。网络、模型繁忙、超时或限流都可能导致失败；保留手动录入方式，稍后可重试。当前部署配置中，机票和高铁共用每个服务用户 24 小时 10 次、全体用户 20 次的限额，每分钟最多 3 次；云端日期辅助为每用户 20 次、全体用户 50 次。窗口从首次请求开始，失败请求也可能计数；额度可能调整，实际以服务返回为准。The bar shows image preparation, waiting for AI and completion; it is not an exact model-computation percentage. Network problems, busy models, timeouts or rate limits can cause failure. Manual entry remains available, and you can retry later. The current deployment configuration shares 10 ticket requests per service user and 20 across all users per 24-hour window, with up to 3 per minute per user. Cloud date assistance allows 20 per user and 50 overall. Windows start with the first request; failed requests can count. Limits may change; service responses determine availability.
切到票务 App 查看信息后能继续编辑吗？Can I continue editing after checking another app?
正常切到后台再返回时，机票／高铁添加或编辑窗口保留已填信息，可继续输入。完成后请点保存；取消会放弃当前修改。此行为不等于强制退出或系统终止 App 后恢复未保存草稿。During a normal background switch and return, flight/train add and edit forms retain entered fields so you can continue typing. Tap Save when finished; Cancel discards the current changes. This does not promise recovery of unsaved drafts after force-quitting or the system terminating the app.
数据会自动在多台设备间同步吗？Does my data sync between devices?
当前版本不提供 Campus 跨设备云同步。主要记录保存在本机。设置中提供备份导出与恢复；恢复前请阅读确认提示，并自行保管导出的文件。iOS 系统备份由系统设置管理，不等于 Campus 自己提供的实时同步。The current version does not provide Campus cross-device cloud sync. Primary records are stored on your device. Settings offers backup export and restore; read the confirmation before restoring and keep exported files safe. iOS backups are managed by system settings and are not real-time Campus synchronization.
如何删除数据或撤回权限？How do I delete data or revoke access?
可在设置中使用“删除账户与全部个人数据”，删除本机资料，并在有既有服务会话时请求删除关联服务端资料。联网删除失败时，请保留 App 并重试；卸载 App 不等于完成服务端删除。外部导出备份、系统备份和其他设备副本需分别处理。日历、麦克风、语音识别和通知权限可在 iOS 设置撤回；关闭云端辅助会停止新请求，但不会自动删除既有会话。Use “Delete account and all personal data” in Settings to remove local data and, when an existing service session is present, request deletion of linked server records. If online deletion fails, keep the app and retry. Uninstalling does not complete server deletion. Handle exported backups, system backups and copies on other devices separately. Revoke calendar, microphone, speech recognition and notification access in iOS Settings. Disabling cloud assistance stops new requests but does not automatically delete an existing session.
如何反馈 Bug 或提出建议？How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
请发邮件至支持邮箱，说明 App 版本、iOS 版本、设备型号、操作步骤、预期结果和实际结果。截图请先遮挡姓名、私人日程、票据或证件信息。功能建议可以直接描述你想完成的事情。我们没有在线工单平台，也不承诺固定回复时限。Email support with your app version, iOS version, device model, steps, expected result and actual result. Hide names, private schedules, tickets and identity details in screenshots. For a feature suggestion, describe what you want to accomplish. There is no online ticket portal or guaranteed response time.
