Today

先看清今天。Start with today.

查看下一项安排、今天的待办和逾期提醒。当天日程与本月生活费摘要，让你少切换几个页面。See your next event, tasks due today and overdue reminders. Your daily schedule and monthly budget summary bring the essentials into view.

  • 下一项日程与时间Your next event and its time
  • 今日待办、逾期与临近截止Today’s tasks, overdue items and upcoming deadlines

Calendar

给每个安排，
一个清楚的时间。Make room for
what’s ahead.

按日、周或月查看安排。手动添加日程，或在授权后读取你选中的 Apple 日历；也可以导入课表。Browse your schedule by day, week or month. Add events manually, read selected Apple calendars with permission, or import a timetable.

系统日历连接为只读，不修改 Apple 日历中的事件。The Apple Calendar connection is read-only. Campus does not edit those events.

Reminders

记下来。
然后，一件件完成。Write it down.
Take it one task at a time.

设置截止日期、具体时间和优先级。按今天、未完成或已完成查看待办，必要时延后，完成后留下记录。Set due dates, times and priorities. View today’s tasks, open items or completed tasks, postpone when needed, and keep a record of what you’ve finished.

待办由 Campus 在本机管理，不与 Apple 提醒事项同步。Tasks are managed locally by Campus and do not sync with Apple Reminders.

Expenses

知道花了多少。
也知道还剩多少。Know what you’ve spent.
And what’s left.

记录日常开支、查看分类，设置每月生活费额度。多币种记录配合参考汇率换算，让消费信息更容易读懂。Record everyday expenses, review categories and set a monthly allowance. Multi-currency entries and reference conversions make spending easier to understand.

参考汇率需要联网获取，换算在本机进行，不代表银行实际结算金额。Reference rates require a network connection. Conversion runs on device and may differ from bank settlement amounts.

智能添加Thoughtful assistance

说出安排，
由你决定保存。Put a plan into words.
You decide what to save.

自然语言输入先由本机规则解析；支持的设备还可使用 Apple 设备端模型。识别结果成为可编辑草稿，核对后再保存。Natural-language input starts with local rule parsing. Supported devices can also use Apple’s on-device model. Results become an editable draft for you to review before saving.

可选云端日期辅助需要开启并确认发送，只传输预览中的日期词语、当前日期和时区。服务或额度不可用时，仍可手动添加。Optional cloud date assistance requires enabling it and confirming a request. Only the previewed date words, current date and time zone are sent. Manual entry remains available when the service or its quota is unavailable.
